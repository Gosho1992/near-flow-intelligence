NEAR Flow Intelligence — institutional holdings, on-chain flows and token supply for NEAR

Bitwise's NEAR ETF, rebuilt from the chain.

Every day we add up the staking accounts that hold the NRR ETF's NEAR, check the total against Bitwise's own published figure, and set its moves next to NEAR's new supply and the protocol's buyback fund.

Follow the NEAR

Pick a creation or a redemption and follow it account by account. Every step links to the transaction on NearBlocks. Each step says how sure we are: "observed" is a transaction by that account; past the custody hub, NEAR is pooled, so a link is at best "probable" (the only matching outflow within 72 hours) and never certain.

    What this proves, and what it doesn't

    Shown directly by the chain

    • The NEAR staked by the accounts our rule attributes to NRR, every day.
    • Every creation and redemption transaction, with amounts.
    • Where redeemed NEAR went next, hop by hop.
    • NEAR's total supply, the NEAR minted each epoch, and total stake.
    • The buyback fund's balance, including its wNEAR inside NEAR Intents.

    Not shown by the chain

    • Whether NEAR moving into custody was bought on the market, or delivered from existing holdings.
    • Whether redeemed NEAR was sold. Trades inside an exchange are not on-chain.
    • Legal ownership of the accounts. Our attribution is rule-based and checked against Bitwise's figures.
    • Executed buyback purchases or burns. We show the buyback fund's balance only.
    • Other NEAR investment products, which are not yet attributed on-chain.

    Check it yourself

    Everything on this page is rebuilt from cached public chain data by one command. No API key, wallet or paid service.

    git clone https://github.com/Gosho1992/near-flow-intelligence
cd near-flow-intelligence
make data    # rebuild every number offline from data/raw
make test    # 18 checks, incl. the match with Bitwise's figures
make serve   # this page on http://127.0.0.1:8000
