# Sources

Every number in this project comes from one of the sources below. Chain data is cached (gzipped) in `data/raw/`, keyed by a hash of each request, so `make data` rebuilds everything offline from the same answers.

| Source | What it establishes | How it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| NEAR archival RPC, `https://archival-rpc.mainnet.fastnear.com` | Blocks and total supply (block headers), account balances (`view_account`), staking-pool balances (`get_accounts`), validator stakes (`validators`), protocol inflation settings (`EXPERIMENTAL_protocol_config`), wNEAR balances in `intents.near` (`mt_balance_of`) | Every request and answer in `data/raw/rpc/`, with the block height in the request |
| FastNEAR transaction API, `https://tx.main.fastnear.com/v0` | Each account's transactions, with receipts, transfers and logs | Every request and answer in `data/raw/tx/`; the last page of each history is re-fetched on refresh |
| Bitwise NRR holdings page, `https://nrretf.com/` | Issuer-reported "NEAR in Trust", NEAR per share, market value, NAV, shares outstanding | `data/issuer_snapshots.csv`: as-of date, retrieval time (UTC) and note for each of the 3 snapshots (29 Oct, 9 Nov, 11 Nov 2026) |
| Bitwise NEAR ETF prospectus, SEC Form 424B3 dated 24 Sep 2026, `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002067111/000121390026103106/ea0306486-424b3_bitwise.htm` | Prospectus Summary: creation and redemption in baskets of 10,000 shares, in cash or in kind, only through pre-approved authorised participants; Coinbase Custody as NEAR custodian (segregated accounts, cold storage); staking through staking agents chosen by the sponsor (Attestant, a sponsor affiliate, may be one); staking expenses of 33% of rewards; target of 100% staked | Sections cited: Prospectus Summary, "The Trust's Investment Objective and Strategies" (baskets of 10,000 shares; cash and in-kind creation; in-kind or cash redemption), "Plan of Distribution" (pre-approved authorised participants), "Custody of the Trust's Assets" (Coinbase Custody), "Staking" and "The Trust's Fees and Expenses" (Staking Expenses equal 33% of staking rewards; about 100% of NEAR staked at the outset). Cited in THESIS.md (evidence against) |
| Google BigQuery, public dataset `bigquery-public-data.crypto_near_mainnet_us` | Independent completeness check: the same 94 pool transactions as the pipeline, hash for hash | Query in `data/bigquery/completeness.sql`; result (counts and SHA-256 of the sorted hash lists, run 13 Nov 2026) in `data/bigquery/completeness_result.json`; checked by test 19 |
| Buyback-fund definition, nikolaskahris-png/near-thesis-data (GitHub) | Which accounts count as the buyback fund: `buybacks.multisignature.near` and `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`, native NEAR plus wNEAR held in Intents | METHOD.md §7; `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near` excluded as the front-end revenue account shown on revenue.near.org |
| SVRN, Inc. press release, 27 Aug 2026 (SEC exhibit `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1869467/000091957426005810/p15060228_ex99-1.htm`) | SVRN's reported treasury: more than 55.5 million NEAR, with institutional staking and qualified custody | Context only in THESIS.md, dated 27 Aug 2026 |
| SVRN pool observation: NEAR archival RPC, `get_number_of_accounts` and `get_accounts` (pages of 100) on `svrn_bitwise.poolv1.near` at the latest daily height | Delegators of SVRN's Bitwise pool and their staked balances: a partial view of SVRN's treasury | Code `nfi/institutional.py`; every account and balance in `data/work/institutional.json` (with date and block height); raw answers cached in `data/raw/rpc/`; summary row in `data/out/institutional_holders.csv` |
| 21Shares NEAR Protocol Staking ETP (Trackinsight, 24 Sep 2026); Grayscale NEAR Trust S-1 | Other NEAR products, for context only | Not attributed on-chain |
| Issuer-data ETF trackers: CryptoKeyhole (`https://cryptokeyhole.com/etf-flows/all/`), SoSoValue | Existing trackers that report NRR holdings and flows from Bitwise's own files (CryptoKeyhole: from 23 Oct 2026) | Positioning only: this project rebuilds holdings from the chain instead |
